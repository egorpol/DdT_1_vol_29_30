# Changelog

All notable changes to this edition are recorded here. Version numbers will follow the [encoding profile](docs/mei-encoding-profile.md#release-identity-versions-and-citation) once a release is prepared: patch releases for compatible corrections or metadata fixes that keep event IDs, minor releases for added works or coverage, and major releases for changes to event identity, segmentation or analytical interpretation. Musical corrections are listed even when the version step is small.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Hasse, Konzert für Flöte, zwei Violinen, Viola und Bass (`hasse_flute_concerto`), assembled on 2026-10-27 from the 29 page files corrected by Jingyu Tu (July–August 2026, with a second pass over pp. 33–37 on 2026-10-25): one `mdiv`, 421 measures in three movements (Allegro non molto mm. 1–115, Largo e moderato mm. 116–211, Allegro mm. 212–421), 29 facsimile surfaces with measure zones, and the publication header from the start. The second system of 24 pages, encoded with a second `pb`, begins with an `sb`; 362 IDs repeated from earlier pages (281 of them figured-bass `harm` IDs `fb_1`, `fb_2` … restarting on each page) take page-qualified IDs; `@ppq`/`@dur.ppq` are dropped as in Pisendel. The 421 measures match the page files otherwise. Status `combined`; listed in the editorial work list and the viewer. Its consistency report (CAMAT 0.2.7) has 64 rows: 29 IIIF Schematron warnings and their summary row, 5 Verovio beam-placement warnings, 4 rhythm and layer warnings (mm. 201, 307, 358, 409), 16 single layers numbered 5, 8 dynamics-spelling notes, and one `space` without an ID (m. 112, typed as `xml:base`).
- Pisendel publication header (MEI 5.1 CMN profile, `meiversion="5.1+CMN"`): work title, GND-linked composer, responsibilities of CAMAT, Francy Häring and Egor Polyakov, CC BY 4.0 data licence, the DdT 1907 source with its BSB identifiers and pp. 1–32, application history, editorial declaration, principal work `work.pisendel_violin_concerto` (D major) linked from the `mdiv`, and a revision history back to the page-level correction. No edition number is set until a release includes the work. The review gate's DdT publication warnings for the file fall from 17 to 2 (event IDs on two `dir`, Schematron warnings pending review).
- A web viewer in `site/`, ported from DdT 1 vol. 11's: each assembled work rendered by Verovio page by page beside its BSB facsimile page, measure zones linked both ways, links such as `#pisendel/37`, the editorial notes, and two remembered display settings (mark every note target, box every measure). Measure boxes cover the measure's staves only, as in the Webern Op. 27 viewer; a box around everything drawn in the measure, as in vol. 11, reached more than 50 units beyond the staves in 126 of Pisendel's 480 measures. The seven works are listed in printed order; those not yet assembled show as pending. `scripts/build_site.py` assembles it and a GitHub Pages workflow publishes it.
- `scripts/check_site.py`, a headless check of the built or published viewer: every page of every work, with rendered measures against their zones, facsimile images, measure boxes and console errors. It needs Playwright, which `requirements.txt` leaves out.
- Validation layer ported from DdT 1 vol. 11: `scripts/validate_corpus.py`, editorial-report refresh, CAMAT update helper, `validation/` profile and work lists (Pisendel), pytest suite with a synthetic publication-profile fixture, CI review gate, and the corpus-check / facsimile-viewer notebooks. Pins CAMAT 0.2.7.
- Pisendel, Konzert für Violine und Streichorchester (`pisendel_violin_concerto`), assembled by Egor Polyakov on 2026-09-02 from the 32 page files (commit `dd634c6`): one `mdiv`, 480 measures, 32 facsimile surfaces with measure zones. Francy Häring corrected the page files (July–September 2026) and continued correcting in the combined file until 2026-10-07. Status `combined`; the consistency report, regenerated with CAMAT 0.2.7 on 2026-10-27, has 104 rows after the publication header and staff-label fix (120 before; the previous hand-made report had 206).
- Pages 00032, 00033 and 00052 (printed pp. 2, 3, 22) re-encoded by Egor Polyakov from Dorico MusicXML and given IIIF facsimiles and measure zones (2026-09-01/02).
- Documentation and license layer: README restructure, `docs/editorial-workflow.md`, `docs/editorial-policy.md`, `docs/mei-encoding-profile.md`, `docs/templates/mei-header-template.xml`, pinned `schemas/mei-CMN-5.1.rng`, `LICENSE.md` with `LICENSES/`, and `CITATION.cff`.

### Fixed

- `metcon="false"` removed from the 63 measures that still carried it (Pisendel 33, Hasse 30), all inherited from the page files: every layer of each fills its meter and each contains every declared staff, so CAMAT's consistency check reports the same findings with and without them. DdT 1 vol. 11 removed its 96 such markers in the same way.
- Hasse rhythm, layer and ID warnings, checked against the DdT page images by an AI model (OpenAI Codex) and spot-checked by the editor: the last two chords of the beamed group in m. 307 (staff 8) changed from quarters to the printed eighths, the printed eighth rest added in m. 409 (staff 6), the stem-down voice in m. 358 (staff 8) renumbered from layer 1 to 2, the printed lower voice of m. 201 (staff 8), which ends after two beats, padded with a quarter `space`, 16 single layers renumbered from 5 to 1, and the `space` in m. 112 given the `xml:id` that its page file had typed as `xml:base`. The five Verovio mixed-beam warnings (mm. 22, 85, 105, 111) are kneed beams printed as encoded and stay. The review gate's Hasse findings fall from 34 to 29 (all IIIF Schematron warnings; 68 to 63 in all) and its consistency report from 64 to 43 rows.
- Pisendel rhythm and staff warnings, checked against the DdT page images by an AI model (OpenAI Codex) and spot-checked by the editor: five spurious spacers removed after complete printed measures (mm. 71, 83, 181, 208, 216), the printed triplets in mm. 92 and 94 given `numbase="2"`, the printed eighth rest added in m. 383 (staff 10), staff placeholders renumbered or added for unprinted staves (mm. 242, 398–400), slur `sxzterk2` moved to staff 3 (m. 376), and the directions "Fagotti soli" and "Tutti." given xml:ids. `metcon="false"` was removed from the eight corrected measures that now fill their meter. The review gate's Pisendel findings fall from 69 to 34 (32 IIIF Schematron warnings, one subsidiary-voice layer in m. 230, one summary row) and the consistency report from 104 to 69 rows.
- Pisendel's 61 printed groups of three 32nd notes, one per eighth in 17 measures in 3/8 (mm. 218–460), keep their printed values and are enclosed in hidden 3:4 tuplets so that each fills its eighth, as is the subsidiary voice in m. 253 that shares two of their noteheads; the reading is documented in the edition's first editorial annotation (`editorial.pisendel.three-32nd-groups`, label `encoding-note`) and in `editorialDecl`.
- Pisendel staff 3 label: "Violino conertato." corrected to the printed "Violino concertato." (p. 1), which clears the consistency report's staff-label-variant finding for staff 3.
- Pisendel m. 37 (p. 4): restored the 3/4 meter of the Vivace after the common-time Andante (mm. 27–36); without it mm. 37–173 were read as 4/4 and CAMAT 0.2.7 reported 924 underfull layers (`64ee4df`).
